# F3 Q Builder: small-group preview guide

Target preview: Friday, October 23, 2026. Staging only: https://studio-ux-streamline--fe-workout-planner.netlify.app/

The October 22 candidate includes the new vibe-first flow, icon choices, an autosaved working Q sheet and direct **Make a Weinke** authoring. See [WEINKE.md](WEINKE.md) for the latest verified deployment. Engineering verified private saving, cloud acknowledgement, hosted desktop/mobile WebKit recovery, printing and Lead. See `ENJOYABLE_FLOW.md` for the exact deployed commit and verification. The latest real AI probe succeeded for two creation cases and targeted refinement, but one creation case timed out. Creative generation is not guaranteed. Offline templates and manual editing remain available.

## First workout (no account required)

1. Choose **I have an idea** to describe your Q, or **Help me find one** for inspiration. The inspiration path asks for a vibe and AO, then offers three short concepts. Browsing uses no AI.
2. Under **Anything else?**, choose coupons **Yes/No** and running **Lots/Some/None**. Add an optional note, then **Build my workout**. **Use offline template** is also available, including while AI is working. Unknown AO features are not promises of availability; confirm today’s conditions yourself.
3. Review the entire workout in order. Tap **Edit [exercise]** for reps, movement instructions, counting or order; simple edits use no AI. **Help me change this** provides optional creative refinement. Template output may need manual changes to match a selected concept or theme.
4. The working Q sheet saves automatically. **Saved on this device** confirms the browser copy; **Saved to cloud** separately confirms upload. Retry a failed save before leaving. **More editing options** opens the established advanced editor, which still has explicit Save controls. Formal Builder/import remain under **Other ways to start**.
5. Choose **Lead this workout**, **Print workout**, or **Copy for Slack**. You can also reopen it from **My Workouts**. Lead starts with a readable sheet; **Session timer** and **Intervals** are optional.

If a previous draft opens first, choose **Back to setup → Choose another starting point** to see the new welcome. Your saved workout stays in My Workouts.

## Already have your workout?

Choose **Make a Weinke**, the third welcome option. Enter The Thang in your own words; name, warmup, Mary and COT/reminders are optional. Keep counts, rounds and counting rules in your notes. **Preview my Weinke → Save & open Weinke** puts it in My Workouts and opens the phone sheet without starting a timer. This path makes no AI calls and does not guess exercises or timing. Notes recover after refresh in the same browser session; use Save before closing the browser. **Start a new Weinke** clears only the form after confirmation and keeps saved workouts.

## Optional account and return visit

An account is optional for planning. For a profile and private account storage, select **Keep my workouts**, then **Create account**; enter your **F3 Name** and email. The signup message uses an **email confirmation link**. Returning users use the separate **email sign-in code** flow. These are not interchangeable.

When opening a saved-workout bookmark, the app waits for library recovery and opens it when available. If you started as a guest, follow the guest-workout import option rather than rebuilding your workout. To check recovery choices, use **Saving & recovery**. Never send private recovery codes as shared workout URLs.

Session countdowns and intervals pause when you leave Lead mode; use **Resume** when you return. **Join live … schedule** follows the clock even after leaving. Sound is optional. If screen wake is declined, keep the screen awake manually. A failed AI refinement leaves the previous draft intact; retry later or deliberately choose a template.

## Before the Q

Confirm the AO and any seasonal venue selection, weather, surface condition, equipment, route and shelter with local leadership. A map link identifies an official default location, not a verified landmark, safe route or facility. Save and open the workout before heading outdoors; verify any offline copy while connected.

## Known limits for preview participants

- AO amenities, Site Q names and Nation locations are **reviewed snapshots**, not automatic live synchronization. Some information remains unknown or pending Site Q review.
- AI availability and quality depend on the free eligible providers; a template fallback is different from generated output.
- Cloud acknowledgement is distinct from a device save. Offline copies remain device-dependent until successfully synchronized.
- Shane completed the short phone acceptance test and separately verified email delivery. Continuous locked-screen audio/timing, installed voices and wake-lock behavior remain device-dependent; keep Lead visible when using timers or audio.
- Shared/public workout publishing and a public AO editing system are not part of this preview.
