# Coordinated pilot UX sweep

Shane approved the five improvements together. This builds on the three welcome choices, vibe-first discovery, steel-blue palette and start-fresh recovery work. It preserves one application and the established staging path.

## Changes

- Lead opens on a compact workout sheet. Timer selection lives behind **Timer options**; the timer algorithms, recovery and optional voice controls remain unchanged. Counting conventions appear beside exercises. Dismissible catalog/save notices appear in document flow inside Lead, rather than floating over instructions.
- The working generated Q sheet supports explicit movement-guide swaps and removal alongside existing reps, counting, timing and ordering edits. **Undo last edit** retains up to 20 local edit steps for the open sheet, preserves its current private saved identity and stops applying after a new AI plan arrives. Undo is session-local and resets on refresh. A generated block keeps at least one exercise to remain compatible with refinement validation. Swaps preserve reps/counting and tell the Q to review counts, equipment and terrain.
- My Workouts cards identify saved workouts and show AO (or **AO not set**), edited date and direct **Lead / Edit / Duplicate** actions. Library sources still use the established personal-copy path; a saved detail labels that action **Duplicate workout**. Original content remains separate.
- Make a Weinke keeps plain notes as the default. An optional disclosure adds Thang blocks, literal rounds, exercises, counts, instructions and reordering. Notes are preserved before added blocks. No AI, automatic parsing or timing inference. Structured-only Thangs work too. The account-scoped form recovers blocks after refresh; saving adapts them to existing version 1 blocks and instructions. Existing notes-only sessions and backups remain compatible. An unreadable structured recovery record blocks saving and remains untouched.
- Offline review retains the original idea and offers explicit **Try AI again / Use this offline template**. Retrying asks before replacing open template edits, and a failed retry preserves the current draft. Failed refinement retains the requested change and has **Try again / Keep working on this draft**. Provider routing, keys and free-only policy are unchanged.
